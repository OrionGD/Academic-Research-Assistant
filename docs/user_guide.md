## **🌐 Overview**

ARAS (Academic Research Assistant System) is an AI-powered tool designed to help researchers, students, and academics manage their research workflow. The system combines document management, semantic search, and conversational AI to streamline your research process.

### **Key Features**
- 📚 **Document Management**: Upload and organize research papers, books, and notes  
- 🔍 **Semantic Search**: Find information using meaning-based search across all your documents  
- 💬 **AI Research Assistant**: Ask questions about your documents and get intelligent answers  
- 📑 **Citation Generation**: Automatically generate citations in various styles  
- 🔗 **Knowledge Graph**: Visualize connections between concepts and documents  
- 📊 **Research Analytics**: Get insights about your research patterns and topics

## **🚀 Getting Started**

### **1. Account Setup**
- **Sign Up**: Create an account using your email or institutional credentials  
- **Profile Setup**: Add your research interests, field of study, and preferences  
- **Institution Link**: Connect to your academic institution for enhanced features  

### **2. Initial Configuration**
1. **Set Research Goals**: Define what you're working on (thesis, paper, literature review)  
2. **Configure Preferences**:
   - Preferred citation style (APA, MLA, Chicago, etc.)
   - Language preferences
   - Notification settings
3. **Import Existing Research**:
   - Connect to reference managers (Zotero, Mendeley, EndNote)
   - Import from cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive)
   - Bulk upload existing PDF collections
